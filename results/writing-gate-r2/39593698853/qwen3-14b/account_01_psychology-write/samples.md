**psychological_mechanism:** In the heat of conflict, the brain’s survival instincts kick in. A part of the nervous system called the dorsal vagal complex may activate, slowing the body down, dimming awareness, and disconnecting from the present. It’s a primal response—an attempt to avoid harm by withdrawing, not fighting. It’s not about being passive. It’s about being overwhelmed. This can leave someone feeling physically numb, as if their body is stepping out of the scene to protect their mind from what’s happening.

**realization:** This is not weakness. It’s not a choice. It’s a reflex. A self-protective reflex that can feel like failure. But understanding this can help you recognize when someone else is not choosing silence—it’s their body speaking in a language you may not yet know.
